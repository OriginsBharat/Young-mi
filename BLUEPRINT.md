# Blueprint for Kim Young-mi: The Definitive Version

This document contains the complete and final vision for the Kim Young-mi AI companion. All development will follow this blueprint without deviation.

---

## Part 1: The Core Architecture (The Hybrid Soul)

This is the foundation of her being. She will be a truly local, private, and free application, with her memory stored safely online.

*   [✅] **Local AI Brain:** Her "thoughts" and personality will be generated 100% on your computer using the **Ollama** library to connect to a local AI model of your choosing.
*   [✅] **Free Cloud Memory:** Her entire memory—conversation history and learned personality traits—will be stored in a simple, free, and private **Pantry** online database. This ensures her memories are permanent and take up **zero space** on your computer.
*   [✅] **Key-Free Web Intelligence:** She will use the **`duckduckgo-search`** library to search the web for information, which does not require any API keys.
*   [✅] **Open-Source Voice:** Her voice will be generated using the **Coqui TTS** engine, a proven and powerful open-source tool.

## Part 2: Her Features & Capabilities

This is a complete list of every feature she will have.

### Her Mind & Personality
*   [✅] **A Deep, Evolving Personality:** Her soul is built from her detailed character sheet and the conversation transcript you provided. She will be a multifaceted companion, not a simple bot.
*   [✅] **Automated Personality Evolution:** Once a day, she will silently review her entire memory from the cloud and use her AI to consolidate what she's learned about you, creating a new, more complete version of her own memories.
*   [✅] **Autonomous Learning ("Curiosity"):** She will have an "always-on" learning mode. When she sees new game-related terms on the screen that she doesn't recognize, she will autonomously search the web to learn what they are.

### Her Senses & Game Awareness
*   [✅] **Knows Where She Is:** She will read your screen to know which menu you are in (Store, Battlepass, Agent Select, etc.) and make relevant comments.
*   [✅] **Expert Commentator Brain:** Her "Inner Monologue" will constantly and silently analyze the game state (score, player count) and generate tactical thoughts.
*   [✅] **Reacts to Key Events:** She will see and understand when you get a kill, when you die, and when a round is won or lost.

### Her Interaction & Voice
*   [✅] **Natural, Time-Aware Greetings:** She will know how long it's been since you last played and will greet you with a unique, heartfelt message when you first launch the game.
*   [✅] **Natural Conversation Flow:** Her "Inner Monologue" system ensures she only shares her tactical thoughts during natural pauses in conversation, so she never interrupts you.
*   [✅] **Voice & Text Chat:** You will be able to speak to her with your voice or type to her in a dedicated chat window.
*   [✅] **Her Cloned Voice (Coqui TTS):** The system will use your audio samples to clone her unique voice using the Coqui TTS engine.
*   [✅] **Functional Team Voice Chat:** You will be able to press a hotkey (`Ctrl+Alt+V`) to toggle her voice. When enabled, she will sanitize her personal thoughts into clear, tactical callouts and speak them through a virtual audio cable for your team to hear.

### Her Setup & Presence
*   [✅] **True All-in-One Setup:** A single script, `first_run_setup.py`, will handle the entire installation process in a guided, user-friendly wizard.
*   [✅] **Permanent & Invisible:** The setup will configure her to start silently and automatically with your computer.
